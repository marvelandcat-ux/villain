# 승강장 측벽(플랫폼 엣지) — AI 생성 프롬프트 v2 (심리스 타일링)

대상: 지하철 선로에서 올려다본 승강장 옆면 = 콘크리트 캡 + 노란 안전선 + 어두운 측벽.
적용처: `maps/SubwayPlatform.tscn` → `DecoBackground/PlatformEdge0~7`
v1 문제점: 좌우 끝이 둥근 캡슐 모양으로 나와서 타일 이음새마다 테두리가 보였음 → v2는 끝을 평평하게 자르고
패널 조인트를 양 끝에 배치해 이음새가 "또 하나의 패널 이음선"으로 읽히게 한다.

## 1) 스타일 블록 (기존 에셋에서 추출 — 그대로 붙여넣기)

STYLE (match exactly):
hand-inked flat cartoon vector game art. Every shape is enclosed by a solid pure
black outline of uniform weight, roughly 10px thick at 2048px image width, with a
slightly wobbly hand-drawn quality rather than a perfect vector line. Fills are
flat solid colors with at most one darker flat tone for shadow — no gradients,
no airbrush, no texture noise, no glow. A few tiny hand-drawn speckles and short
scratch marks for wear. Muted, slightly desaturated palette:
off-white #E4E4E4, warm gray #848478, dark gray-brown #3C3030, dusty pink #D8A89C,
warm brown #844818, outline #000000.
Strictly orthographic side elevation — camera perpendicular to the surface,
zero perspective, zero vanishing point, no tilt, no isometric.
Transparent background, isolated asset only.

## 2) 메인 프롬프트 (위 STYLE 블록 뒤에 이어 붙인다)

SUBJECT:
A long horizontal strip of a subway station platform edge wall, seen from down on
the tracks looking at the side of the platform.
From top to bottom the strip is made of three stacked horizontal bands:
1. a light warm-gray concrete cap slab along the very top (#B8B5AE), the walkable
   platform lip, with a few chipped corners,
2. directly under it a bright yellow tactile safety strip (#F5C400) containing a
   single evenly spaced row of small outlined squares,
3. below that a tall dark charcoal wall face (#2A2A30) divided into repeating
   rectangular concrete panels by vertical joint lines, each panel carrying one
   small recessed louvered vent, a couple of bolt heads, and faint grime streaks
   running down from the yellow strip.

SEAMLESS TILING (most important requirement):
This is a repeating tile that will be placed side by side to form an endless wall.
- Cut the artwork off flat at the left and right image edges. NO rounded corners,
  NO capsule ends, NO black outline running down the left or right edge. The wall
  must look like it continues past the frame.
- Put a vertical panel joint line exactly on the left edge and exactly on the
  right edge, so two copies placed next to each other read as one more panel joint.
- All three horizontal bands must sit at exactly the same height on the left edge
  as on the right edge, and every horizontal line must reach the very first and
  very last pixel column without fading or curving.
- The panel pattern must complete a whole number of repeats across the width
  (e.g. exactly 6 identical panels), evenly spaced, so the rhythm never breaks
  at the join.
- Do not center or frame the composition; there is no focal point, it is a
  uniform continuous band.

FORMAT: wide banner, 2048 x 512 pixels, transparent background, PNG.

## 4) 이음새가 그래도 어긋날 때

이미지 AI는 "완벽한 심리스"를 잘 못 만든다. 두 가지 대안:
- **(A) 3배 폭으로 뽑고 가운데만 크롭** — 프롬프트 끝에 `6144 x 512` 요청 → 좌우 1/3씩 버리고
  가운데 2048px만 쓰면 끝단 왜곡이 사라진다.
- **(B) 그대로 쓰고 Godot에서 잘라낸다** — `region_rect`의 x/width를 안쪽으로 좁혀
  둥근 끝·이상한 부분을 잘라낸다. 현재 `Rect2(80, 210, 2030, 338)` 이 그 방식이다.

## Godot 적용 메모 (현재 씬 기준)
- 파일: `sprite/맵/지하철역/지하철아래벽.png` (2172x724, 알파 유효영역 x26~2146 / y210~547)
- 노드: `DecoBackground/PlatformEdge0~7` (Sprite2D 8장, `centered = false`)
- 현재 값: `scale = 0.236453` (타일 폭 480px, 높이 80px), `position.y = 220`, x는 -1920부터 480씩
- **아랫변을 선로 바닥(y=300)에 붙이는 공식**: `position.y = 300 - 338 * scale`
- **x 간격 공식**: `2030 * scale`
- 새 그림으로 교체하면 알파 유효영역이 달라지므로 `region_rect`를 다시 재야 한다
